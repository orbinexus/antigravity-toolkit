# Catering & Banquet Quotation Engine

This skill guides the quotation, per-head package calculation, and contract generation for large banquets and corporate catering.

## 1. Cost & Pricing Calculation

1. **Tiered Per-Head Pricing**:
   - `Total Package Cost = (Per Head Rate * Guest Count) + Service Charges + Equipment Rental + Venue Fee`.
2. **Staged Milestones**:
   - Deposit 1 (Booking confirmation): 30% to 50% advance token.
   - Deposit 2 (T-minus 7 days): Final guest count freeze and 40% payment.
   - Final Settlement (Event day): Remaining balance.

## 2. Automated Proposal PDF
- Generate branded, multi-page catering proposals detailing menu choices, staffing, schedule of events, and terms of cancellation.
