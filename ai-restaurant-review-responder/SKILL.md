# AI Restaurant Review Responder & Reputation Sentinel

This skill directs automated sentiment analysis, personalized response generation, and service recovery workflows for customer reviews.

## 1. Sentiment Classification & Response Strategies

1. **5-Star & 4-Star Reviews (Appreciation & Retention)**:
   - Generate warm, personalized gratitude mentioning specific dishes reviewed (e.g. *"Thrilled you enjoyed the Truffle Burger and seasoned fries!"*).
   - Avoid generic copy-pasted templates; vary tone across cheerful, professional, and friendly.
2. **1-Star & 2-Star Reviews (Service Recovery)**:
   - Acknowledge customer frustration empathetically without accepting legal liability.
   - Route instant high-priority alert (SMS / WhatsApp) to Store Manager with order ID and phone number.
   - Provide direct offline contact: *"We would love to make this right. Please WhatsApp our GM directly at +1-XXX-XXX."*
