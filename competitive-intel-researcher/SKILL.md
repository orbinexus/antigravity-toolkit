# Competitive Intelligence & Market Researcher

This skill directs systematic competitive benchmarking, product gap discovery, pricing model analysis, and customer feedback synthesis.

## 1. Research Protocol

1. **Competitor Discovery & Profiling**:
   - Identify direct competitors, indirect alternatives, and category incumbents.
   - Profile core value propositions, target customer personas, and tech stack indicators.
2. **Feature Matrix & Gap Analysis**:
   - Construct comprehensive feature comparison matrices.
   - Identify neglected customer pain points, feature holes, and usability complaints from public reviews.
3. **Monetization & Pricing Breakdown**:
   - Map pricing tiers, free/freemium allowances, usage-based metrics, enterprise add-ons, and hidden fees.

## 2. Strategic Synthesis Output
- Produce executive-level reports with:
  - **Feature Parity Table**: Where your product stands vs top 3 competitors.
  - **Unfair Advantage Opportunities**: High-impact differentiators with low implementation cost.
  - **Actionable Roadmap Recommendations**: Prioritized quick wins and core bets.
