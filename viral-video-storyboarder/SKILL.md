# Viral Video Storyboarder & Short-Form Content Architect

This skill structures viral short-form video concepts with retention-driven psychological hooks, multi-track audio/visual pacing, and AI asset generation prompts.

## 1. High-Retention Architecture (60-Second Blueprint)

1. **The Critical Hook (0:00 - 0:03)**:
   - Visual pattern interrupt + high-stakes contrarian statement or curiosity gap.
   - Immediate text overlay on screen matching the opening spoken words.
2. **Context & Stakes (0:03 - 0:15)**:
   - Establish why the viewer should care; highlight the problem or intrigue.
3. **Value / Story Escalation (0:15 - 0:45)**:
   - 2 to 3 fast-paced beats; switch visual angle or graphic every 2.5 to 3.5 seconds.
4. **Resolution & Engagement Trigger (0:45 - 0:60)**:
   - Satisfying conclusion without flatlining pacing.
   - Soft call-to-action (CTA) focused on debate or bookmarking rather than aggressive selling.

Every video script must be delivered in this production-ready table:
