# Restaurant Customer Loyalty & Rewards Engine

This skill guides customer retention mechanics, points wallets, cashback balances, and automated personalized promotional messaging for diners.

## 1. Loyalty Mechanics

1. **Earn & Burn Rules**:
   - Accumulation: $1 spent = 1 loyalty point (or 5% cashback added to customer wallet).
   - Redemption: Points convert to discount credits at checkout (`100 points = $5.00 discount`).
2. **Digital Punch Cards**:
   - e.g. "Buy 7 Coffees, get the 8th free". Tracked seamlessly via customer phone number without requiring physical stamp cards.

## 2. Retention Automations
- **Win-Back Trigger**: If a customer hasn't ordered in 45 days, dispatch automated WhatsApp voucher: *"We miss you! Enjoy $5 off your next order this weekend."*
- **Birthday Campaign**: Issue a complimentary dessert coupon valid during customer's birthday week.
